Campaigns Dulux Spruce. Brand launch

Show Your True Colours.

For the first time in 95 years, Dulux colour names were made in Lagos.

ClientDulux Spruce
RoleVP Creative and Communications, UACN
Year2026
MarketNigeria

The brief

A new Dulux brand, built for young Nigerians at a price within reach. But the colour names came from somewhere else. Neptune Seas. Wholemeal Honey. Lovely words. Hollow ones.

The idea

For the first time in the brand’s 95 year history, colour naming left global headquarters and came home.

Colour psychologists, consultants, Gen Z voices and pop culture minds met in one room in Lagos. Ten colours. Ten names Nigeria already speaks. Each one cast as a character and shot by one of the country’s foremost fashion photographers.

The build

Made in-house at UACN. Digital, outdoor and film.
