B-Sides X3M Collective. All staff album

The Xperiment.

An ad agency made an album. Eight tracks. One staff directory.

ClientX3M Collective
RoleOne of the artists
Year2023
MarketNigeria

The idea

X3M Ideas turned ten. So the team made an album.

The first body of work in Nigeria made only by the employees of a creative agency. Produced by Steve Babaeko, written and performed by the people who usually write the ads.

The record

Eight tracks of Afro-fusion, about 24 minutes, from head bopping bangers to laid back mid tempo songs. Videos were shot for Tanana, Soco, Cypher, Flex and Broken No More. Released on 27 January 2023.
