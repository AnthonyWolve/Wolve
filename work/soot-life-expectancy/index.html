RoleCreative Director, X3M Ideas

The idea

We recreated Nigeria’s life expectancy around the soot problem.

What happened

Nigeria’s first Cannes Lion in 70 years, and the first for a Creative Director from West Africa. Grand Prix at the Pitcher Awards and the Luum Awards. A Silver at the Loeries. A shortlist at D&AD.

The story continued as Soot City, a documentary Wolve co-produced.
