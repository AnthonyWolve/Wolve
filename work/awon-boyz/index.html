The film

Lagos calls them area boys. This film asked who they are.

A 38 minute documentary by Tolu Itegboje that follows eight young men across Oshodi, Monkey Village and the New Afrika Shrine. Their dreams, their fears, their hustle.

The run

Selected for iREP, the Slum Film Festival and Lights Camera Africa in 2019. Premiered on Netflix in April 2021, then went global. Wolve worked on the promotion.
