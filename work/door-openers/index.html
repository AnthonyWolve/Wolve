Door Openers.

A governor’s aide had a door opener. So did his door opener. We made it a challenge.

ClientOriginal skit
RoleCo-creator
Year2021
MarketNigeria

The spark

A video of Governor El-Rufai’s convoy went round Nigerian social media. One aide opened a car door for another aide, who then opened the door for the governor. Doors, opened for the people who open doors.

The idea

So we opened doors for the door openers.

A parody of the chain, conceived with a colleague. It went viral instantly.

What happened

The top blogs reposted it. Within days it became the #OpenDoorChallenge, recreated by influencers and celebrities including Bovi, and picked up by news outlets like SaharaReporters.
