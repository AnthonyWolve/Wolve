Escape 2020.

Everyone wanted out of 2020. So we made it a game.

ClientX3M Ideas
RoleDeputy Creative Director, X3M Ideas
Year2020
MarketNigeria

The brief

Christmas 2020. COVID restrictions tightened and families were told to stay home for the holidays. Nigerians agreed on one thing: they could not wait to see the back of the year.

The idea

Instead of Christmas cards, a board game.

Escape 2020 squeezed the year into 365 blocks, one for every day. Lockdowns, looting, #EndSARS, the Beirut blast, wildfires. Every big moment of the year, local and global, waiting to be dodged with a roll of the dice.

Sent to clients and friends of the agency. Now everyone could escape 2020, at least in their heads.
