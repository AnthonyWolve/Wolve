The idea

Not an ad. A system, from product to pop culture.

The Camon 50 is a camera phone. So the launch had to live where Nigeria watches: on feeds, in group chats and in its film culture. One idea, rolled out in stages, each one handing the audience to the next.

The rollout

  1. Blackout Protocol. Nigeria’s first branded social show. Six episodes of action thriller, built to drive pre-orders.
  2. Launch. Four short films that turned the specs into a spectacle.
  3. Beyond the Specs. A two minute Nollywood short. Story first, specs second. It went viral.
  4. Culture Co-Signs. Creators telling it their way.
  5. AI meets In-House. AI made content to test new formats, read and answered in real time by the in-house team.

What happened

Strong pre-orders. A strong launch week. Every sales target hit.
