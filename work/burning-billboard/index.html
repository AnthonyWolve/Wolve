Campaigns FCCPC. Don’t Burn Their Future

Burning Billboard.

Nigeria’s first burning billboard. A child’s dream, going up in smoke.

ClientFCCPC
RoleCreative Director, X3M Ideas
Year2024
MarketNigeria

The brief

Tobacco kills more than 8 million people a year, by WHO’s count. In Nigeria the warnings on packs keep getting more graphic, and young people keep starting anyway. The Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission needed adults to stop it.

The insight

The future belongs to the young. Smoking cuts it short.

Today’s kids are tomorrow’s CEOs, founders and doctors. Start smoking early and the ambition burns with the cigarette.

The idea

We took it to the format Nigeria loves most. A 26 episode Nollywood telenovela about the dangers of smoking, backed by TV, outdoor and social.

And Nigeria’s first burning billboard. Children’s dreams, literally going up in smoke. Fire ran through every execution, so nobody could scroll or drive past.
