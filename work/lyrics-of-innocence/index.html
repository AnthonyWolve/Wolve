Lyrics of Innocence.

Kids heard the hits. They missed the dirty bits. That was the point.

ClientNirvana Water
RoleCreative Director, X3M Ideas
Year2023
MarketNigeria

The insight

Children and Nirvana Water share one thing. Purity.

But the songs kids hear every day are full of explicit lyrics, and that exposure can shape them long before they understand it.

The idea

A social experiment, launched on Children’s Day. Seven kids were asked to explain the lyrics of popular songs. They missed every vulgar meaning. Their innocence made the case better than any warning could: protect it before they know.
