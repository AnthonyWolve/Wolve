Power Without the Wait.

Who should sell a phone that lasts for days? The people who live without power.

ClientTECNO
RoleChief Creative Officer
Year2026
MarketNigeria

The brief

A mid range phone. A social first budget. We didn’t guess the hero feature. We asked. Research across three metropolitan states put battery first by a wide margin: about two days on one charge.

The insight

The most credible voice for a product is the person who needs it most.

Anyone can say a battery lasts. Someone who gets a few hours of power a day can prove it.

The idea

So we hired them. Chief Power Ambassadors. A real nationwide job vacancy, run with HR and our in-house content team, open to anyone getting under six hours of power a day.

Five ambassadors got the phone two weeks before launch to test the claim in their own lives.

What happened

One ambassador posted his journey unprompted. Not paid. Not briefed. Only on his own page. Nearly 300K organic views in two days.
