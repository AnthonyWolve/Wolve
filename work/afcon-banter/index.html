AFCON Banter.

Rights landed one day before kickoff. So we skipped the commentary and went for the banter.

ClientDStv × AFCON
RoleCreative Director, X3M Ideas
Year2024
MarketNigeria, Ghana and South Africa

The brief

DStv secured the AFCON rights one day before kickoff. No time for a big campaign. Every day of the tournament still had to count.

The insight

Every AFCON team is already an animal.

Super Eagles. Indomitable Lions. Elephants. Fans were halfway to the joke.

The idea

So the tournament became an animal kingdom, one meme at a time, match by match, with generative AI posters in the mix.

Then came the final. Eagles against Elephants. We dropped Elephant in the Eagle’s Shadow, a nod to a kung fu classic.
