Anthony Eigbe. Most people call him Wolve.

He is Chief Creative Officer for TECNO Mobile West Africa, and has spent 15+ years building African brands across agencies, experiential and in-house teams.

At X3M Ideas he led Soot Life Expectancy, Nigeria’s first Cannes Lion in 70 years, and became the first Creative Director in West Africa to win one. At UACN he ran one creative engine for brands from Dulux to Debonairs. Today he leads TECNO’s work across the region, from launches to AFCON.

Off the clock, he teaches marketing and creativity in public to close to 30K followers. He co-produced Soot City, a documentary, and made an album, The Xperiment, as part of a group.
